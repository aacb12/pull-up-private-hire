Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who we are

Pull Up Private Hire (“we”, “our”, “us”) is the controller of your personal information.
Email: puotl@outlook.com
Phone: +44 7741 405694

2. Information we collect

When you ask for a quote or book a journey, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Journey details: pick-up and drop-off addresses, dates and times, number of passengers and luggage
  • Flight numbers, so we can track arrivals and adjust pick-up times
  • Any other information you choose to give us, such as a request for a child seat
  • Payment and booking records
  • Communication records (calls, messages, emails)

3. How we use your information

We use your personal data to:

  • Give you a quote and arrange your journey
  • Contact you about your booking, including on the day of travel
  • Process payments and issue receipts
  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Comply with legal obligations, including private hire licensing requirements

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your information under one or more of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract (your booking)
  • Legitimate interests (customer service, keeping booking records)
  • Legal obligations (accounting, insurance, licensing)
  • Consent (for any marketing messages, which we only send if you ask for them)

5. Data retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, typically up to 6 years for accounting and insurance purposes.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share it with:

  • Payment processors, if you pay by card
  • Our insurers, if there is a claim
  • The licensing authority, police or other authorities, if required by law

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise your rights, contact puotl@outlook.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access.

9. Our website and cookies

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not set any cookies of its own.

  • The quote form does not send your details to us directly. It opens WhatsApp or your own email app with your message ready, and nothing is sent until you choose to send it.
  • The map in our contact section is provided by Google Maps. Google may set cookies when the map loads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them.
  • Our website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

10. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always appear on our website.
