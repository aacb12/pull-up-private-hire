FAQ

Common questions

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can, especially for early-morning flights and long journeys. We have one car, so booking ahead is the best way to make sure we can take you. For a last-minute trip, message us anyway and we’ll tell you straight away if we can do it.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number when you book. We track your flight and move your pick-up to when you actually land.

How many passengers and bags can you take?

Up to 4 passengers and 3 large check-in suitcases. If you’re travelling with more people or luggage, message us before booking and we’ll tell you honestly what fits.

Is the price fixed?

Yes. We agree one price for the whole car before you travel, so there’s no meter running. If an airport drop-off or parking charge applies, we’ll tell you when we quote.

Can you take me there and bring me back?

Yes. Choose Return on the quote form and we’ll price both journeys. It’s popular for holidays: we drop you at the airport and collect you when you land.

Do you go to England?

Yes. As well as Scottish airports and towns, we do long-distance journeys to Newcastle, Manchester, London and anywhere else in the UK.

Can we stop on the way?

Of course. On longer journeys, just let us know and we’ll plan comfort and food stops.

Do you provide child seats?

Tell us your children’s ages when you ask for a quote and we’ll let you know what we can provide.
